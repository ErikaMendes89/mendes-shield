# Code of Conduct

Mendes Shield aims to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Critique implementations and proposals; do not target people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and encouraging unauthorized security testing. Participants should keep discussions relevant, explain disagreement in good faith, and respect the privacy and safety of others.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately using the reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include exploit details in a public issue.
