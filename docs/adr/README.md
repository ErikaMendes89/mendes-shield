# Architecture Decision Records

[Home](../../README.md) · [Architecture proposal](../architecture/README.md) · [Roadmap](../roadmap.md) · [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

ADRs record significant technical decisions and their consequences. They distinguish an approved decision from a candidate discussed in the architecture proposal.

No integration ADRs have been accepted yet. Record decisions before adding ingestion, storage, queues, models, or WAF adapters. Do not treat candidate technologies in the architecture document as selected dependencies.

## Decision record format

Each ADR should include:

1. **Title and status:** proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected.
2. **Context:** the problem and constraints that require a decision.
3. **Options:** alternatives considered and why they were not selected.
4. **Decision:** the chosen approach, once approved by the project owner.
5. **Consequences:** operational, security, privacy, maintenance, and rollback impacts.
6. **Verification:** measurable acceptance criteria and evidence required before completion.
